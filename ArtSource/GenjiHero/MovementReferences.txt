Implementation interpretation (new animation, not extracted Blizzard clips):
Movement: short, low ninja stride; knee lift, reverse stepping and lateral weight shift; animation phase follows travelled distance.
Double jump: a 0.58 second visual forward somersault with tucked knees and unfolding recovery, initiated only by a successful air jump. This timing is authored for this project, not measured from the reference videos.
Landing: brief knee compression and return to movement. Attack/dash arm poses take priority.
Visual rotation affects the body only, not the collision capsule, aim or camera. Successful second jumps are synchronized to other players with a network timestamp.
